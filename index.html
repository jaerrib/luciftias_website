New EP-length single!

Kammermaschine-27.3

Kammermaschine-27.3 is now available on Bandcamp, Funkwhale, and Archive.org. This is the third entry in a four-part exploration of the space where computer science, alchemy, and the occult converge. Filtered through ambient drone and shaped by the stark geometry of German Expressionist film, it represents citrinitas, the dawning of illumination and the emergence of inner radiance from the work of transformation.

The first two releases in the series, Kammermaschine-27.1 and Kammermaschine-27.2, are also available:
